AUREL — Independent automotive visualization study.

Ferrari 458 Italia model by vicent091036:
https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6
Distributed in the Three.js car materials example:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/webgl_materials_car.html
Original geometry retained; materials, lighting, surface droplets, and camera/render pipeline adapted.

Blender Cycles used to render the film. Intel Open Image Denoise used for guided denoising. AgX used for color management. Foreground rain/mist and depth haze are compositing.

Live fallback studio HDRI: Studio Small 03, Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_03
